# Down Attack

**Down Attack** is an open-source **Black Desert Online (BDO)** PvP toolkit featuring an empirical **PvP Damage & Combo Calculator** and a real-time **Arena of Solare Stream Overlay** for OBS Studio.

---

## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ PvP Damage & Combo Calculator (`/calculator`)

- **Side-by-Side Combat Simulation:** Compare Player 1 vs. Player 2 simultaneously with full stats (Sheet AP/AAP, Total PvP AP/AAP, Accuracy, Melee/Ranged/Magic DR & Evasion, Base DR %, HP, and Special Attack modifiers).
- **Empirical BDO Formulas:** Built on verified BDO PvP mechanics and empirical formulas by **@gpw**, including the 70/30 AP split, class-vs-class modifiers, special attack multipliers (`Down Attack`, `Back Attack`, `Air Attack`, `Critical`), and the **7% minimum damage floor**.
- **Interactive Effective HP (HPE) Curves:** Visualize DR and Evasion survival curves, marginal gains (`+10 DR` vs. `+10 Evasion`), and exact DR/Evasion breakpoints.
- **4-Slot Combo Builder:** Build, reorder, and compare up to 4 skill combos per player with hit-by-hit configuration, class buffs/debuffs, and estimated combos-to-kill.
- **Garmoth Import & Presets:** Import character builds directly from Garmoth, load official Arena of Solare presets, or log in with Discord to save and share custom presets and combos.

### 🎥 Arena of Solare Stream Overlay (`/solare`)

- **Live OBS Browser Widget:** Real-time stream overlay supporting **Vertical (`340×520 px`)** and **Horizontal (`790×200 px`)** layouts with transparent background support.
- **Multi-Region Support:** Search and track players across **SA, NA, EU, KR, JP, RU, TR, and TW**.
- **Session & Win Streak Rating Tracking:** Tracks live session Win/Loss record, net rating gain/loss (`PTS`), recent match deltas, combat averages (Avg Damage, Damage Taken, CC, Recovery, K/D), and official consecutive win-streak bonus points (`+2`, `+5`, `+8` PTS).
